## Design System

### Colour Palette
The application uses a modern, healthcare-inspired colour palette designed for clarity, high contrast, and a premium aesthetic.

<p align="center">
  <img src="medicines/static/images/Colour Palette/med-compare-color-palette.png" alt="MedCompare Colour Palette" width="800">
</p>

The palette was chosen to evoke trust and cleanliness, with strong distinction between primary actions (Blue/Teal) and neutral backgrounds (Slate/Ice).
